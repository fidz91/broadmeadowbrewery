If you try to brew a **36 IBU Czech Premium Pale Lager** using typical hard British tap water, the high hop load will scrape across your palate like aspirin. The magic of a true Bohemian *Světlý Ležák* lies in the interplay between **ultra-soft water** (`14 ppm Calcium`, `18 ppm Sulfate`, `22 ppm Chloride`), a simple **95.5% Floor-Malted Bohemian Pilsner / 4.5% Carahell** grist bill, and patient cold fermentation.

## Why Keep the Grist at 95.5% Base + 4.5% Carahell?

In earlier batches of **Meadowgrass Pils**, we experimented with adding `5% Munich I` and `2% Melanoidin Malt` to fake a decoction character. While tasty, those malts pushed the color into deep copper-gold (`6.5 SRM`) and left a heavier German *Märzen*-like sweetness.

Switching to **95.5% Weyermann Floor-Malted Bohemian Pilsner** and **4.5% Carahell**—combined with pulling a single thick decoction between our `62.5°C` and `71.0°C` rests—gave us the exact pale straw-gold radiance (`3.8 SRM`) and toasted bread-crust aroma we were chasing.

## Fixes After Our Silver Medal at Midlands Craft Open

At the **Midlands Craft Brewing Open (June 2026)**, Batch #039 took **Silver (`41.0 / 50`)** in a 34-beer lager flight. Both National judges praised the Saaz herbal aroma and rich malt depth, but noted a faint wisp of young lager sulfur on the initial swirl.

For **Batch #047**, we made two targeted process refinements:
1. **Extended Maturation Rest**: Kept the beer at `13.5°C` for an extra 48 hours *before* sealing the spunding valve so natural $\text{CO}_2$ evolution could scrub volatile $\text{H}_2\text{S}$ completely.
2. **6 Weeks Horizontal Lagering at $-0.5^\circ\text{C}$**: Transferred under closed pressure into a purged keg and lagered for 42 days prior to tapping on our new **LUKR side-pull faucet**.
