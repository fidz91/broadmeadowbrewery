Autumn is officially my favorite season in the Broadmeadow brewhouse. Chilling groundwater temperatures have dropped back down to `11.5°C` (meaning our immersion chiller brings `21 L` of boiling wort to pitching temperature in under 14 minutes), and the annual shipment of **freshly kilned 2026 Herefordshire & Kent whole-cone hops** has landed on the doorstep.

## 3 Kilograms of Whole-Cone Goldings, Fuggles & Bramling Cross

This week we spent two hours portioning and vacuum-sealing our 2026 harvest boxes into `35 g` and `50 g` mylar bricks for the deep freezer (`-22°C`):
- **East Kent Goldings (`5.4% AA`)**: Bright, resinous, with an unmistakable sweet lavender and marmalade nose.
- **Herefordshire Fuggles (`4.4% AA`)**: Earthy, minty, and damp-woodland fresh—destined for our winter Dark Milds and Best Bitters.
- **Bramling Cross (`6.1% AA`)**: Hedgerow blackberry and blackcurrant leaf, earmarked for our Christmas Export Stout.

## Upgrading Tap 01 to a Czech LUKR Side-Pull Faucet

After visiting several lager-focused taprooms earlier this summer, we finally swapped the standard forward-sealing faucet on **Tap 01** for a **Czech LUKR side-pull faucet**.

Unlike a traditional top-lever tap that opens vertically, the LUKR uses a side-actuated ball valve and a long stainless micro-screen nozzle submerged directly below the beer's surface:
- **Crack the valve 15% open**: The screen shears dissolved $\text{CO}_2$ into dense, wet, creamy micro-foam (*mokrá pěna*) that tastes subtly sweet and protects the beer below from oxidation.
- **Open the valve 100% underneath the foam cap**: Crystal-clear lager flows smoothly beneath the meringue head without agitating the beer.
