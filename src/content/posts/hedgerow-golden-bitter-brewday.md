There is nothing quite like opening a vacuum brick of freshly kilned whole-cone **East Kent Goldings** at 07:30 on a crisp October morning. For **Batch #046 ('Hedgerow' Golden Bitter)**, our goal was to implement the exact adjustments we noted after taking Bronze (`39.5/50`) at the Three Counties Spring Classic earlier this year: keep the **89.0% Simpsons Golden Promise** / **6.0% Carahell** / **5.0% Torrefied Wheat** grist intact, but shift a generous charge of whole cones into a cooler `76°C` post-boil whirlpool steep to lock in volatile lemon-thyme and orange marmalade aromatics.

## Brewday Timeline & Gravity Readings

- **07:15 — Strike Water Prep**: Collected `29.5 L` of RO water and dosed Gypsum (`CaSO₄`), Calcium Chloride (`CaCl₂`), and Epsom Salt (`MgSO₄`) to hit `170 ppm SO₄ : 85 ppm Cl`.
- **07:50 — Mill & Mash-In**: Milled `4.16 kg` total grain at `0.95 mm` gap. Dough-in hit `66.1°C` right on target. Measured room-temperature mash pH at 15 minutes: **`5.29 pH`** (target `5.30`).
- **09:10 — Vorlauf & Sparge**: Pre-boil gravity measured **`1.036 SG`** across `27.2 L` (up `1.5%` mash efficiency over Batch #035 thanks to a slower 35-minute lauter).
- **10:30 — Whirlpool Hop Steep**: Rather than throwing our late Goldings in at flameout (`100°C`) where delicate linalool and geraniol flash off, we chilled the kettle down to **`76.0°C`** first, then steeped `35 g` of whole-cone Goldings in the mesh hop spider for 20 minutes.

## Lessons on Whole-Cone Wort Absorption

One practical reminder whenever switching from T-90 pellets to whole leaf hops: whole cones soak up roughly **`5.5 mL to 6.5 mL` of wort per gram of hops**. With `95 g` of whole-cone Goldings in the kettle, we lost roughly `0.6 L` more wort to the hop bed than usual, landing at **`20.8 L` into the fermenter at `1.043 OG`**.

> "Dropping the kettle to 76°C before adding the final 35g of whole-cone East Kent Goldings preserved a vivid, floral honey-and-bergamot aroma that survived vigorous primary fermentation."

## Fermentation & Conditioning Notes

Pitched **Imperial A09 Pub** at `17.5°C`. High krausen arrived within 14 hours with a thick, whipped-cream yeast cap. By Day 4, gravity reached `1.010 SG`, where we sealed the spunding valve to capture the last point of fermentation for natural conditioning (`1.8 volumes CO₂`). Currently pouring on **Tap 02** at `9.0°C`—bright, snappy, and dangerously drinkable.
