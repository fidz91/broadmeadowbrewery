There is nowhere to hide in a `1.038 OG` Dark Mild. When a beer finishes at 3.6% ABV, any flaw in grist balance, mash temperature, or water chemistry immediately shows up as a watery mid-palate or acrid roastiness. Over the past eighteen months at Broadmeadow Brewery, **Bramble & Bark** has been our most frequently re-brewed recipe—and the beer that taught us the most about recipe formulation.

## The Evolution Across Four Batches

When we brewed **Batch #018 (v1)**, we made the classic mistake of treating a Dark Mild like a scaled-down Robust Porter. We mashed at `65.5°C` and used `7%` standard Chocolate Malt (`450 °L`) alongside medium crystal. The beer attenuated down to `1.007`, leaving a dry, slightly husky finish that clashed with the low alcohol.

| Iteration | Batch | Mash Temp | Sugar / Crystal Approach | Yeast Strain | Key Sensory Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **v1** | #018 | 65.5°C | 8% Crystal 60L, 7% Chocolate | S-04 Dry Ale | Too dry (`1.007 FG`); acrid roast edge on the finish |
| **v2** | #027 | 67.5°C | 8% DRC, 4.5% Pale Chocolate | WLP002 English | Much smoother cocoa note, but slightly one-dimensional sweetness |
| **v3** | #042 | 68.5°C | 8.5% DRC, 5.5% Invert #3, 4% Pale Choc | Wyeast 1469 | **Gold Medal (43.5/50)** — stone fruit, plum, walnut, velvety finish |
| **v4** | #049 | 68.5°C | 8.5% DRC, 5.5% Invert #3, 3.5% Pale Choc | Wyeast 1469 | Current cellar batch; dialed Pale Choc down 0.5% per judge notes |

## Why Homemade Invert No. 3 Changes Everything

It seems counterintuitive to add `5.5%` simple sugar to a low-gravity beer when you are trying to preserve body. However, when you pair **Homemade Brewers Invert No. 3** (made by slowly cooking raw demerara cane sugar with citric acid at `115°C` for two hours) with a high `68.5°C` mash temperature, magic happens:

1. **Flavor Concentration**: Invert No. 3 contributes dark fig, date, and subtle molasses aromatics without adding the cloying caramel heaviness of excessive high-Lovibond crystal malts.
2. **Grist Balance**: By replacing a portion of the base malt with invert sugar and compensating with **6.0% Flaked Torrefied Barley** and **8.5% Simpsons Double Roasted Crystal (DRC)**, the finished beer stays crisp on the swallow while maintaining a rich, creamy head from first pour to the bottom of the nonic glass.

> "The secret to a world-class Mild isn't piling on specialty grain—it's pairing a high-temperature Maris Otter mash with chloride-forward water, Pale Chocolate capped late, and an expressive Yorkshire top-cropper."

## Capping the Roast Malts & Water Chemistry

For **Batch #049 (v4)**, we kept our signature `135 ppm Chloride : 65 ppm Sulfate` water profile with `35 ppm Sodium`. That touch of sodium acts like a pinch of sea salt on dark chocolate—it wakes up the **3.5% Thomas Fawcett Pale Chocolate** and **8.5% Simpsons DRC** immediately on the front of the tongue.
